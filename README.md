I'm an Electrical Engineering student focused on embedded systems and PCB design. I build custom hardware and firmware — from microcontroller-based devices to fully designed PCBs — with hands-on experience across the STM32 and ESP32 ecosystems. My portfolio reflects practical, end-to-end projects spanning circuit design, embedded firmware, and sensor integration, with a growing interest in robotics.
